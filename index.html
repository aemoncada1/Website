        Questions Owners Ask

        Straight answers before you pick up the phone.

        Will anyone find out my business is for sale?
        No. We never list your business publicly. Every prospective buyer signs a confidentiality agreement before your company is identified, and your employees, customers, and competitors remain unaware until you decide otherwise.
        What is a Broker’s Opinion of Value, and how is it different from an appraisal?
        A Broker’s Opinion of Value is a market-based view of what your business would likely command from qualified buyers today, prepared by an advisor who negotiates with those buyers regularly. A certified appraisal is a formal valuation used for legal, tax, or lending purposes. If your situation requires one, we will tell you so and point you to the right resource.
        Do I need to be ready to sell before contacting you?
        Not at all. Many of the owners we work with are one to three years away from a sale. A business assessment tells you where you stand today and what would move value — and often the best advice is to prepare first.
        What kinds of businesses do you work with?
        Owner-operated, privately held businesses across trades and services, healthcare, technology, real estate, retail, and e-commerce. We work nationwide from Miami and bring qualified buyers from across the U.S. and Latin America.
        Do you also represent buyers?
        We represent owners. Working exclusively on the sell side means our only obligation in a transaction is to you, and our buyer relationships exist to serve your sale, not the other way around.
        How do fees work?
        Plainly and up front. We discuss fees in our first conversation, and there is no obligation of any kind until you decide to engage us.
        How quickly will I hear back?
        A senior advisor will contact you privately, typically within one business day.
