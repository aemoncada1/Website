        What this estimate can’t see

        • Recurring revenue. Service agreements, contracts and repeat customers raise what buyers will pay.
        • How much runs through you. If key customers, licenses or relationships depend on you, buyers discount for that risk.
        • Customer concentration. One customer above 15–20% of sales worries buyers and lenders.
        • Financing. Many buyers borrow to buy, often with an SBA loan. If a lender won’t finance the price, the price usually comes down. We can check this before you go to market.
        • Add-backs. Personal or one-time expenses run through the business can raise your earnings, and your value, when they’re documented.
